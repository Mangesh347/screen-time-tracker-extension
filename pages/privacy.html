Privacy Policy

Screen Time Tracker by Fenwick Labs · Last updated: 2026-09-10

We built this extension to help you understand and control browsing time. Tracking data stays on your device by default.

What we store locally

  • Website domains and time spent (not page content, passwords, or form data)
  • Your settings, limits, focus lists, and optional theme preferences

Account & cloud (optional)

  • Google Sign-In via Supabase Auth (email, name, avatar)
  • Pro entitlement and optional settings sync when you upgrade
  • Payment processing by PayPal or Razorpay — we do not store card numbers

Permissions

We use tabs, storage, idle, alarms, notifications, and identity for tracking and login. Host access is used to measure active sites and apply focus/cleaner features.
